Where to grab a box

The common boxes in US grocery aisles. Each button opens that store's search, so you see the price and stock near you. H-E-B is in Texas only. Walmart and Amazon both deliver.

Box sizes. Recipes assume a standard 15.25 oz (432 g) box. Some Betty Crocker boxes are now 13.25 oz. With those, use about 2 tablespoons less liquid or oil, or add ⅓ cup flour. Raw mix. Like raw flour it can carry bacteria, so the no-bake recipes heat-treat it first: microwave on a plate in 30-second bursts, stirring, until it reaches 160 °F (71 °C).
